The story

I was you.

Five months ago I was 193 pounds. Bagels every morning. French fries whenever I could. A bag of Doritos and a Coke watching football on Sunday — which isn't even food when you actually think about it. Tired by 2pm every day. I blamed it on travel. On stress. On age.

I was wrong on all three counts.

"Clean food and clean thinking are the same practice. What goes in your body and what goes in your mind — that's the whole game."

My doctor gave me a number I couldn't rationalize away. I sat in my car in that parking lot and made a decision. No Ozempic. No trainer. No $300 program I'd quit by week three. Just six honest decisions about what I was putting in my body — and what I was letting into my head.

167 pounds today. More energy in my 50s than I had at 40. I travel constantly for work. I'm not perfect. I'm just consistent enough that it worked. And I wrote down exactly what I did.
