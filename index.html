The truth

Nobody told me
it was this simple.

I spent decades eating whatever was convenient. Bagels every morning. Bread with everything. Sugar in the coffee, sugar in the sauce, sugar in things I didn't even know had sugar. I told myself I was busy. I told myself it was age.

"What goes in your body and what goes in your mind — that's the whole game."

Five months ago I got a wake-up call. No dramatic diagnosis. Just a moment of honesty with myself about what I was putting in my body — and what I was letting into my head.

I cut the white bread. I dropped refined sugar. I started intermittent fasting — eating in a 6-hour window, fasting 18. I stopped buying ultra-processed food. That's the whole list.

167 pounds today. More energy at 55 than I had at 40. Not because I found a hack. Because I stopped lying to myself.

Big Food doesn't want you to know how simple this is. Simple doesn't sell programs. Simple doesn't need a subscription.

The method

Four things.
That's all it was.

01

No refined carbs

White bread, bagels, white rice, pasta. Gone. Not reduced — removed. Your body doesn't need them. It just got addicted to them.

02

Dramatically less sugar

Not zero. Dramatically less. Read every label. Sugar hides in everything. Once you see it, you can't unsee it.

03

Intermittent fasting

18 hours fasting. 6-hour eating window. Your body was designed to fast. We just forgot.

04

Clean thinking

What you consume with your mind is as important as what you eat. Noise, fear, outrage — these are ultra-processed food for the brain.
